**The image:** a paper rocket launches from a child's bedroom, bursting
through the ceiling into a vast starfield — Earth, Saturn, nebulae and
asteroids above, warm lamplight and toys below. One clear focal shape (the
origami rocket on its glowing trail), a scale twist from a tiny room to the
infinite cosmos, and strong upward momentum.

**Why this one:** of three candidates (sky-whale over a city, aurora serpent
over a cliff, paper rocket), the rocket has the most original idea — a
handmade toy breaking out of an ordinary bedroom into space — rather than a
stock surreal scene. It reads clearly even at ~300px: rocket, glowing trail,
bedroom.

**How it works:** `main.ts` (Node, stdlib only) copies the bundled
`entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`) as `entry.png`.
Deterministic — the same bytes every run. No network, no dependencies.

**Entry:** `entry.png`, 683 × 1024 px, 367,321 bytes (limit: ≤1024×1024,
≤1,048,576 bytes).
